OUR STORY

From a mother's kitchen, now in your city.

Dastarkhwan began as a small street-corner dhaba, where the smell of the grill would call the whole neighbourhood over. We still do things the old way ― meat cooked slowly over wood coals, dough rolled by hand, and spice blends only our family knows.

We don't just serve food ― we lay out a gathering, where every guest feel like one of the family.
